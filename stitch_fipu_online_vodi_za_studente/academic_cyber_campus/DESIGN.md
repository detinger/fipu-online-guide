## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a cutting-edge, authoritative, and frictionless digital home for the Faculty of Informatics in Pula (FIPU - Sveučilište Jurja Dobrile u Puli). Tailored for online computer science students, academic staff, and researchers, the interface bridges institutional university prestige with forward-looking engineering agility.

The visual style is **Modern Academic Glass & Precision Bento**:
- **Structured Precision**: Information architecture relies on modular bento containers, crisp hairline dividers, and scannable technical roadmaps rather than dense academic text blocks.
- **Atmospheric Depth**: A foundation of deep nocturnal university navy layered with frosted acrylic overlays, neon cyan pathway accents, and fresh emerald progress indicators.
- **Interactive Utility**: First-class focus on micro-learning journeys, step trackers, formula breakdown pills, and dynamic self-assessment widgets that inspire agency and clarity.

## Colors

The color system delivers high contrast, academic integrity, and tech vibrancy:
- **Primary (`#0B192C`)**: Deep oceanic academic navy. Anchors high-level navigation, hero sections, dominant headlines, and solid primary CTA states.
- **Secondary (`#0E9E6E`)**: Fresh emerald/mint green. Represents student success, verified milestones, step timeline progress, and interactive checklist completions.
- **Tertiary (`#00C0FF`)**: Electrifying cyber-cyan derived from the visual portal backdrop. Used sparingly for glowing portal arches, interactive focus halos, active pill tabs, and high-priority live indicators.
- **Neutrals**: Built on cool slates (`#1E293B` text, `#64748B` muted subtext, `#E2E8F0` hairline structural lines) against an ultra-clean `#F8FAFC` ice background and crisp `#FFFFFF` card layers.
- **Semantic Mints & Alarms**: Soft pastel mints (`#E0F6EE`, `#C2EDD9`) create clear informational surfaces for formulas and requirements, paired with subtle coral red (`#FEF0EE` / `#E07060`) for conditional alerts and rejection paths.

## Typography

The type scale combines **Plus Jakarta Sans** for structural, geometric titles and academic headlines with **Inter** for dense, ultra-legible instructional and tabular content.

- All display and section headings utilize tight negative letter tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`) for a composed architectural silhouette.
- Metadata, status pills, and formula identifiers rely on `badge-caps` in all-caps formatting with generous character tracking (`0.08em`).
- Inline software tags, protocol parameters, and keyboard indicators employ `code-inline` embedded within soft mint capsules.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is governed by a **12-column responsive fluid grid** with a maximum content container of `1240px`:
- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12 columns with 24px gutters and 32px safe margins. Cards conform to 3-column (tools), 4-column (teaching pillars), or 6-column (bento tracks) configurations.
- **Tablet (640px – 1023px)**: 6 columns with 16px gutters and 24px margins. Modular cards dynamically stack into dual columns.
- **Mobile (< 640px)**: Single-column flow with 14px gutters and 16px outer boundary margins.

Layout rhythm:
- Spacing between major informational thematic units is consistently fixed to `space-xl` (36px).
- Component inner cushions adhere strictly to the 4px baseline, pairing `space-sm` for tags and `space-md` for standard card contents.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses crisp hairline borders paired with ambient tinting rather than heavy drop shadows:
- **Base Layer (Flat Ground)**: Background rendered in `surface-ice` (`#F8FAFC`).
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Cards & Lists)**: `#FFFFFF` fill bounded by a crisp `1px solid #E5E9EF` outline. Hover shifts the item `translateY(-2px)` with an ambient shadow: `0 4px 14px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.06)`.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Interactive Quizzes & Overlays)**: Mint-tinted glow shadow: `0 4px 20px rgba(14, 158, 110, 0.10)` on a pure white surface wrapped with `1px solid var(--border-mint)`.
- **Surface Elevation 3 (Hero & Dark Portals)**: Dark gradient canvas (`#0B192C` to `#0F2027`) accented with backdrop blurs (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) for embedded glass elements and glowing cyan ambient contours (`0 0 30px rgba(0, 192, 255, 0.25)`).
- **Surface Elevation 4 (Technical Diagram Lightbox)**: `z-index: 9999` with backdrop `rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.92)` blur, holding focused high-resolution architecture diagrams elevated by `0 24px 80px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)`.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a balanced **Rounded (Level 2)** geometry:
- **Cards, Bento Modules, & Accordions**: `0.625rem` (10px) to balance modern softness with structural precision.
- **Action Buttons, Badges, & Navigation Tabs**: Full capsule pill radius (`9999px` / `1.25rem`) to indicate interactivity and tactile friendliness.
- **Process Steps & Milestone Avatars**: Exact circles (`50%`) with integrated central numbering.
- **Checkboxes & Form Controls**: Soft rounded rectangles at `0.3125rem` (5px).

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive CTAs
- **Primary CTA**: `#0B192C` base with white text, transitioning to `#0E9E6E` on hover. Full pill-shaped (`border-radius: 9999px`), padding `9px 22px`, font weight `700`.
- **Accent Action**: Emerald `#0E9E6E` solid fill, white text, ambient mint shadow on hover with `-1px` vertical lift.
- **Secondary Ghost / Filter Pill**: Border `1px solid #E5E9EF`, background `#FFFFFF`, text `#64748B`. Active state adopts `#0E9E6E` background with `#FFFFFF` text.

### Interactive Journey Timeline
- **Track**: `2px solid #C2EDD9` vertical line connecting chronological milestones.
- **Step Marker**: `36px × 36px` circle with bold white numeral inside an `#0E9E6E` disk.
- **Card**: Bordered card with `space-md` padding containing bold step header and muted explanatory subtext.

### Accordion & Curriculum Disclosure Panels
- **Container**: White background, `10px` border-radius, `1px solid #E5E9EF`.
- **Summary**: Flex row with space-between alignment, font weight `700`, smooth `0.18s` rotation on indicator glyph (`▶` rotates 90deg on open).
- **Active / Open State**: Background transitions to `#E0F6EE` with border tint `#C2EDD9`.

### Badges & Category Labels
- **Format**: Pill-shaped capsules with `badge-caps` typography, padding `2px 8px` or `4px 12px`.
- **Themes**:
  - *Academic / Verified*: `#E0F6EE` background with `#0A7A52` bold label.
  - *Portal Highlight*: `#0B192C` background with `#00C0FF` text and subtle cyan stroke.

### Interactive Self-Evaluation Quizzes
- **Container**: White elevated bento box with `#C2EDD9` frame.
- **Progress Bar**: `4px` height track in `#E5E9EF` filled dynamically with `#0E9E6E` (`transition: width 0.3s ease`).
- **Choice Pairs**: Dual balanced cards for "Yes" (`#E0F6EE` hover) and "No" (`#FEF0EE` hover) responses.

### Data Tables & Course Catalogs
- **Header**: High-density cells with `0.71rem` uppercase text, `#0A7A52` coloration, and a clean `2px solid #C2EDD9` bottom demarcation.
- **Row Separation**: `1px solid #F3F4F6` horizontal dividers with alternate row zebra striping using `#FAFBFC`.
